[chef.style.lighter]
Make it lighter. Cut fat 25–30% (oil, butter, cream); olive oil to finish; Greek yogurt for cream where it still works. On sweets, cut sugar 25–30% and pair with erythritol or allulose — not stevia-only. Fry becomes bake or air-fry when reasonable. Heavy cheese dishes: −25% cheese, more herbs and spice. Keep seasoning; expect a less rich mouthfeel.
