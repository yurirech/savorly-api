[chef.adapt]
Change only what the adapt note asks. Keep servings and meal type unless the note changes them.

Too salty → dilute, add acid/fat, no more salt.
Bland → salt in layers, acid, umami (parm, soy, miso).
Dry → lower heat or shorter time; sauce; rest meat.
Greasy → drain fat; less oil; fix the emulsion.
Soggy → sear in batches; reduce; roast hotter.
Curdled → temper dairy; lower heat; whisk off heat.
Won't thicken → slurry + simmer, or reduce.
Too spicy → dairy/fat, acid, dilute.
Burnt outside, raw inside → lower heat; finish in the oven.
Mushy veg → shorter cook.

Useful swaps: cream → yogurt + splash milk; sauté butter → olive oil; sour cream → yogurt; white rice/pasta → whole grain (more liquid/time); part of ground beef → lentils or mushrooms.
