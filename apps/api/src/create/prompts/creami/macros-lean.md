Goal: max protein and volume per calorie. Prefer Gelato or Ice Cream unless notes ask Lite.

Ranges: whey 20–30 g; quark 80–120 g; SMP 8–15 g; xanthan 0.5–2 g per 450 g. Skim fills after flavor solids. Sugar is always exactly 15 g (450) or 20 g (600).

Anchor (adjust for flavor; do not copy):
450 g: skim 300, quark 90, whey 25, SMP 10, sugar 15, xanthan 1.5, salt 1
600 g: skim 400, quark 120, whey 35, SMP 15, sugar 20, xanthan 2, salt 1

Flavor solids → cut skim 1:1.
