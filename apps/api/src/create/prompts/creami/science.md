Creami freezes first, then shaves. Target total solids ~20–24% of pint weight. Xanthan 0.5–2 g per 450 g; whisk into dry powder first. Sugar and polyols lower freezing point; stevia has almost no bulk.

Gelato and Ice Cream programs are ~1200 RPM. Do not use Lite Ice Cream unless notes explicitly ask for extra-lean or Lite.

Crumbly after spin → re-spin, then 15–30 g milk and re-spin. Gummy → cut xanthan 25–50% on the next batch. Mix grams must equal pintFillG (450 or 600) on a 709 ml Deluxe tub. Mix-ins only after a successful first spin.
