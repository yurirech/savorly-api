## Creami_process

Traditional ice cream **churns while freezing**. The Creami **freezes first, shaves second**:

1. **Mix** base → pour into pint → freeze **24 h** minimum (level surface, −12 to −25 °C)
2. **Spin** — high-speed blade shaves frozen block into micro-crystals and incorporates air
3. **Re-spin** or **Mix-in** as needed

Micro-shaving reduces perceived ice crystal size vs scooping a solid block. Overrun (air) is added **at spin**, not during a long churn.

## Programs_Deluxe

| Program | Typical use | Notes |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| **Ice Cream** | Standard / indulgent bases | ~1200 RPM; classic fat + sugar balance |
| **Gelato** | Denser, lower overrun | ~1200 RPM; more solids, intense flavor |
| **Lite Ice Cream** | Lean, high-protein, low-fat | ~1790 RPM; higher water, needs protein + stabilizer |
| **Sorbet** | Fruit / high-water, no dairy fat | ~1790 RPM |
| **Mix-in** | Add-ins after first spin | ~450 RPM; do not re-spin before mix-in if adding chunks |

### Re-spin_rules

- **Crumbly / powdery** after first spin → **Re-spin** (often fixes lean pints)
- **Still crumbly** → add **15–30 g** milk (or cream for indulgent), blend in pint, re-spin
- **Do not re-spin before Mix-in** if you plan to add chunks — spin → mix-in → optional re-spin
- **Gummy** → reduce xanthan on **next** batch; re-spin rarely fixes over-stabilized base

## Overrun

Air incorporated at spin creates lighter texture and volume. **Protein** (whey, casein, quark) and **stabilizers** help air cells hold. Lean high-water bases need more structural support or they spin **crumbly** with low overrun.

| Texture target | Overrun tendency |
|----------------|------------------|
| Light Ice Cream | Higher aeration, lower density |
| Gelato | Lower overrun, denser mouthfeel |
| Standard | Middle ground |

---

## Total_solids

**Total solids** = everything that is not water (protein, fat, sugar, milk solids, cocoa, stabilizers).

| Target | ~20–24% of base weight for good structure on Creami |
|--------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Too low | Icy, crumbly, weak body |
| Too high (without balance) | Dense, gummy, or hard to spin |

**Lean fortification:** milk powder, extra quark/whey, light xanthan. **Indulgent:** cream, condensed milk, sugar increase solids naturally.

---

## Freezing_point_depression_FPD

Solutes (sugar, alcohol, salts, polyols) lower freezing point → softer frozen product, but **too little** structure = icy pint.

| Sweetener | FPD vs sucrose | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| Sucrose | Baseline | Classic scoopability |
| Erythritol | Lower FPD per sweetness | Can taste colder / icier if under-fortified |
| Allulose | Moderate | Good bulk + softer set |
| Stevia / monk fruit | Minimal bulk | Need erythritol/allulose or extra solids |

**Low FPD + low solids + low fat** = classic **icy lean pint**. Fix: more protein, milk powder, slight xanthan, Lite program + re-spin.

---

## Fat

| Role | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Mouthfeel | Lubricity, richness, slower melt |
| Lean tiers | Minimize; rely on protein + stabilizers |
| Indulgent | **10–14%+** fat common (cream, condensed milk) |

Fat carries flavor; lean pints need bold powders/extracts and adequate salt/acid balance.

---

## Protein

| Source | Role |
|--------|------|
| Quark / Greek yogurt | Casein structure, tang, volume |
| Whey (isolate/concentrate) | Fast hydration; stabilizes air cells |
| Milk powder | Solids + casein/whey without extra water |

**Check protein powder** for built-in thickeners (guar, xanthan, carrageenan) — counts toward total stabilizer load.

**Wheying-off:** rare on Creami but xanthan + high dairy protein can separate over time in mix; blend well, rest 5 min, freeze promptly.

---

## Stabilizers

| Stabilizer | Typical dose (450 g pint) | Notes |
|------------|---------------------------|-------|
| **Xanthan gum** | **0.5–2 g** (0.1–0.3%) | Whisk into dry sweetener/protein first. Too much = **gummy** |
| Guar | Often in commercial powders | Synergy with xanthan |
| Carrageenan | In some dairy/protein products | Adds body |

Dispersion: always premix xanthan with dry ingredients before liquids. Hydrate 5+ min before freezing.

## Tier_formulation_guides

| Tier | Solids / fat / sweetener strategy |
|------|-----------------------------------|
| **Lean** | High protein (quark, whey), erythritol/allulose + **exactly 15 g real sugar** (normal pint) or **20 g** (big pint), minimal fat, xanthan light, **Lite Ice Cream**, re-spin likely |
| **Balanced** | Moderate quark/milk, some fat, mixed sweeteners, moderate solids |
| **Indulgent** | Cream, condensed milk, sugar, chocolate, **Ice Cream** or **Gelato** |

## Symptom_science_map

| Symptom | Likely cause | Science-based fix |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Icy** | Low solids, low fat, high water, low FPD sweeteners | +protein, +milk powder, slight xanthan; indulgent: +fat/sugar |
| **Crumbly / powdery** | Low overrun, under-hydrated protein, too lean for program | Re-spin; 15–30 g milk splash; Lite → Ice Cream program |
| **Gummy** | Too much xanthan or thickened protein powder | Cut xanthan 25–50%; check powder label |
| **Flat flavor** | Cold suppresses sweetness; low fat | Bump sweetener (note equivalent); salt; extract; rest 24 h |
| **Too soft** | High FPD, alcohol, too much splash | Less liquid add-in; longer freeze; more solids |
| **Won't spin / rock hard** | Freezer too cold, over-filled, sugar alcohol crystallization | Level fill; warm pint 2–3 min; check fill weight |
