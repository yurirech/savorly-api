## What_a_cake_is

A cake is a **solid foam**: starch + protein matrix trapping air, CO₂, and steam. Batter is low-viscosity; finished crumb 18–28% moisture.

**Central challenge — timing:** gases must expand the batter **before** structure sets. Too early → low volume. Too late → collapse.

## Cake_families

| Family | Examples | Leavening | Fat | Mixing |
|--------|----------|-----------|-----|--------|
| Batter | Layer, pound | Chemical + creamed air | High | Cream fat + sugar, add flour |
| Foam | Sponge, angel food | Egg foam | Low/none | Whip eggs, fold flour |
| Chiffon | Chiffon | Foam + chemical | Moderate oil | Oil batter + folded whites |

**High-ratio:** sugar > flour (tender, collapse-prone). **Low-ratio:** sugar ≤ flour (sturdier, pound-style).

## Ingredient_roles

### Flour
Starch gelatinizes (~60–90 °C) = crumb bricks. Gluten = elastic network; **limit** in cakes (cake flour ~7–9% protein).

### Eggs
Whites stiffen cell walls on heat. Yolks emulsify fat/water. Foam cakes: whipped eggs = primary leavening.

### Sugar
Tenderizes, holds water, delays starch gelatinization, aerates when creamed, browns. Controls oven rise and collapse in high-ratio cakes.

### Fat
Aerates when creamed (solid fat), tenderizes, stabilizes gas cells. Oil: moisture, less aeration.

### Leavening
**Double-acting powder:** ~15% CO₂ at mix, ~85% in oven from ~40 °C. ~**5% powder weight vs flour**. Soda only with acid (buttermilk, yogurt, lemon, cocoa). Leavening useless after crumb sets.

### Liquid
Hydrates flour, dissolves sugar, enables reactions, controls viscosity.

## Phase_mixing

Goals: (1) stable fat/water **emulsion**, (2) **air cells** for CO₂/steam to expand.

- **Creaming:** fat crystals + sugar → air pockets; flour last, minimal gluten
- **Foam:** whip eggs, fold flour gently
- **Curdled batter:** broken emulsion — often still bakes; room-temp eggs help

---

## Phase_baking

**Early (→ ~60 °C):** viscosity drops, CO₂ releases, gas/steam expands, oven spring.

**Late (~60–90 °C):** starch gelatinizes, proteins coagulate → **solid foam**, structure set. Further gas escapes through porous crumb.

**Steam** ≈ 1600× liquid water volume — major leavening force.

---

## Critical_race

```
Mixing → air cells
Heat → CO₂ + steam expand → rise
Starch gelatinizes + proteins set → structure fixed
```

**Collapse:** structure sets after max rise but before crumb supports cooling (common high-ratio). **Dense:** structure sets before enough gas expansion.

---

## Phase_cooling

Gases contract; steam condenses. High-ratio cakes collapse in final bake minutes or on cooling. Staling = starch retrogradation.

---

## Browning

Maillard (sugar + amino acids) and caramelization at surface. Alkaline batters (extra soda) brown faster.

---

## Symptom_science_map

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---------|--------------|
| Dense, low rise | Under-mixed; expired leavening; oven cool; set before expansion |
| Collapsed center | Excess leavening/sugar; weak matrix; oven opened early |
| Tough, rubbery | Over-mixed gluten; too little fat/sugar |
| Dry crumb | Over-baked; too much flour; insufficient fat/sugar/liquid |
| Sunken top | Fruit sinking; uneven heat; underbaked |
| Curdled batter | Cold eggs; liquids added too fast to creamed butter |

## Ratio_targets

Flour = 100% by weight:

| Component | High-ratio / chiffon | Low-ratio |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------|
| Sugar | 100–130% | 80–100% |
| Fat | 40–80% | 100% |
| Eggs | 80–110% | 100% |
| Baking powder | ~5% of flour | 0–3% |
| Liquid | 60–100% | minimal |

## Flavor_liquid_adjustments

| Addition | Compensate |
|----------|------------|
| Fruit purée 50–150 g | −75% purée weight from other liquids; +15–30 g flour if very wet |
| Citrus juice 30 g | −30 g other liquid; account acid in leavening |
| Cocoa 15–30 g | Soda if acid pairing |
| Berries folded | Flour-dust; fold end; max ~150 g/layer |
