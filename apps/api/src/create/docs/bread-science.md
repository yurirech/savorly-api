Yeast-bread science for **1 kg bread machines**. Separate from **baking-science.md** (cakes use chemical leavening; bread uses fermentation).

---

## What_bread_is

Bread is a **gluten network** inflated by yeast CO₂, set by starch gelatinization. Target finished crumb moisture **35–45%**.

**Central challenge:** balance fermentation time, gluten development, and hydration so the dough rises fully before bake sets the crumb.

---

## Bread_machine_process

1. **Knead** — develop gluten (machine does this)
2. **Rest / rise** — yeast produces CO₂
3. **Punch down** — redistribute gas (some programs)
4. **Final rise** — volume before bake
5. **Bake** — set structure, brown crust

Cycle length and rise times vary by program (Basic / French / Fast).

---

## Programs_science

| Program | Cycle lean | Formulation lean |
|---------|------------|------------------|
| **Basic** | Standard times | Enriched OK — sugar, fat, milk powder |
| **French** | Longer, often leaner | Less/no fat; crisp crust; lower sugar |
| **Fast** | Shortened rise | +25–50% instant yeast; slightly warmer water OK |
| **Bake** | Heat only | Pre-shaped dough |
| **Knead** | Mix/develop only | Rolls, pizza, etc. |

### Fast_yeast_boost

Multiply Basic yeast amount by **1.25–1.5×**. Do not use Fast for doughs with **>30% whole wheat** — rise time too short.

## Bakers_percentages

Flour blend = **100%**. All other ingredients as % of total flour weight.

| Ingredient | Typical range (11% AP) |
|------------|------------------------|
| Water | **58–63%** white; **65–72%** if ≥25% whole wheat |
| Instant yeast (Basic) | **1.5–2%** |
| Instant yeast (Fast) | **2–2.5%** |
| Salt | **1.8–2%** |
| Sugar (enriched) | **3–10%** |
| Fat (enriched) | **2–6%** |
| Milk powder | **2–5%** (optional enrichment) |

## AP_flour_user

| Flour | Protein | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| User AP (EU) | **11 g / 100 g** | Default — adequate for machine loaves |
| US AP (reference) | ~10% | Weaker — user flour is slightly stronger |
| Bread flour (not in pantry) | ~12–13%+ | Not required for user's machine bread |

- Machine knead develops enough gluten for standard white/enriched loaves
- Dense crumb → **+15–25 g water** or check yeast/freshness before blaming flour
- High wholegrain: add water, don't assume weak flour
- **No vital wheat gluten** in pantry — compensate with water and limit rye/whole wheat %

## Ingredient_roles

| Ingredient | Role |
|------------|------|
| **Flour** | Gluten structure + starch bulk |
| **Water** | Hydrates gluten; enables fermentation |
| **Instant yeast** | CO₂ production; needs food and time |
| **Salt** | Controls fermentation speed; strengthens gluten |
| **Sugar** | Yeast food; tender crumb; browning |
| **Fat** | Soft crumb, slower staling; optional in French |
| **Milk powder** | Richness, browning, softer crumb |
| **Seeds** | Flavor, nutrition; add with dry ingredients |

---

## Yeast_instant

- **Freshness matters** — expired yeast = no rise
- **Basic:** 1.5–2% of flour weight
- **Fast:** 2–2.5% of flour weight
- Do not double salt to compensate for yeast issues

---

## Hydration_and_gluten

| Symptom in dough | Likely cause |
|------------------|--------------|
| Stiff, won't knead smooth | Under-hydrated — +15–25 g water |
| Too wet, collapses | Over-hydrated or over-yeasted |
| Dense finished loaf | Low hydration, dead yeast, or too much wholegrain for program |

Machine knead time is fixed — adjust **hydration and yeast**, not manual kneading.

---

## Whole_wheat_and_rye

| Flour | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Whole wheat | Up to **40%** of blend; +3–5% hydration vs white |
| Rye | Cap **15–20%** — bran cuts gluten, sticky crumb |
| Blend strategy | Start conservative; increase wholegrain in iterations |

---

## Enrichment_rules

Soft sandwich loaves (Basic program):
- Sugar **5–8%** flour
- Fat (butter/oil) **3–5%** flour
- Milk powder **3–5%** optional

French program: reduce or omit fat; sugar **0–3%**.

## Loaf_defaults

| Loaf | Total flour | Water (60% example) | Yeast Basic |
|------|-------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Medium (~750 g) | ~420 g | ~252 g | ~8 g |
| Large (~1000 g) | ~570 g | ~342 g | ~10 g |

Tune per recipe type; sweet doughs may need slightly less water.

## Symptom_science_map

| Symptom | Likely cause | Science-based fix |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Dense / tight crumb** | Low hydration; dead yeast; too much wholegrain for program | +15–25 g water; fresh yeast; Basic not Fast for wholegrain |
| **Won't rise** | Expired yeast; water too hot; too much salt | New yeast; lukewarm water ~35 °C; check salt % |
| **Collapsed / sunken** | Over-proofed; too much liquid; over-yeasted | Less yeast; less water; shorter rise (try Basic) |
| **Mushroom top** | Too much yeast or sugar; over-proofed | Reduce yeast 10–15%; less sugar |
| **Gummy line** | Under-baked; too much sugar | Longer bake if machine allows; reduce sugar |
| **Dry crumb** | Over-baked; low fat/sugar | Slightly more fat or milk powder |
| **Thick / hard crust** | French program or low fat/sugar | Basic program; small enrichment |
| **Raw center** | Too much liquid; wrong loaf size setting | Match machine size; reduce water |
| **Holes / tunnels** | Over-yeasted; air pockets in load | Reduce yeast; layer ingredients per load order |

Post-bake fixes and machine troubleshooting: **recipe-adapt.md**.
